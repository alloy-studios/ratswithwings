# ratswithwings

RATS WITH WINGS is a 3D pigeon simulator about being a small grey menace in a sunny city plaza. You have 120 seconds and a limited payload. Bomb pedestrians from above — head hits score double and they yell things like "THIS IS A HATE CRIME" — desecrate the bronze General for a fortune, dive-bomb people to snatch their ice cream mid-lick, and set off car alarms just because you can. Eating refills your ammunition, fast hits chain a ×5 chaos multiplier, and cooing summons the local flock to follow you around like a tiny feathered gang. Hit the hot dog cart and the vendor starts hurling buns at you, which is a problem right up until you realize the ones he misses with are free food. Your splats stay on the pavement and in people's hair for the rest of the round, so the plaza slowly fills with evidence. Ranks run from "SIDEWALK NOBODY" to "FLYING RAT SUPREME."
